# Brew Buddies — Logo & Colour Brief

**Status: logo finalised.** Concept — two mugs (terracotta + sage) forming a
heart in steam, "Brew Buddies" wordmark, tagline **"Good people. Great
connections."** — approved. Palette below is now live on the site
(`website/index.html`).

## What it is
Brew Buddies is a "coffee roulette" tool: it randomly shuffles the people in a
friend group, team, or community into small tables of 2–4 and sends everyone
a calendar invite to grab a coffee. No app to install, no Slack required —
you sign up, get matched, show up.

## Personality
Warm, low-key, a little playful. Think **Sunday morning kitchen table**, not
corporate coffee-shop chain. It should feel like something a friend made for
their group chat, not enterprise SaaS. Approachable over premium; cosy over
sleek.

**Words that describe it:** friendly, warm, casual, communal, a little
whimsical, unpretentious.

**Words to avoid:** corporate, moody, luxury, minimalist-cold, tech-startup-blue.

## Audience
Two kinds of people set this up: someone running it for their whole company
(HR/culture lead wanting informal cross-team connection) and someone running
it for a friend group or community with no formal structure at all. The
brand needs to feel welcoming to both — approachable enough for a friend
group, credible enough that a company will put its name on it.

## Current visuals (for contrast)
The existing site uses a muted, vintage espresso-bar palette — dark espresso
brown, dusty cherry red, muted gold, parchment cream. It reads more like an
old-world coffeehouse than a Sunday brunch. **The new direction should be
noticeably more colourful and lighter on its feet** — same coffee DNA, more
warmth and energy, less "dark wood and brass."

## Direction: "friendly coffee-morning" palette
Think golden-hour light through a kitchen window, terracotta mugs, honey in
tea, a sage plant on the windowsill. Warm and inviting, not neon — colourful
through warmth and saturation, not brightness for its own sake.

Suggested palette (starting point, not gospel):

| Role | Colour | Hex | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Terracotta | `#E4693D` | the brand colour — warm, energetic, appears in the logo mark |
| Secondary | Golden Honey | `#F5B942` | sunlight, warmth, used for highlights/accents |
| Accent (fresh) | Sage Mint | `#9DBEA0` | a breath of freshness against all the warm tones — the "plant on the counter" note |
| Accent (playful) | Berry Pink | `#E8567A` | small pops of energy — badges, hover states, illustration details |
| Background | Warm Cream | `#FFF6E9` | soft, warm neutral, not stark white |
| Ink / text | Deep Roast Brown | `#4A2E1E` | keeps the coffee identity in the type colour even as everything else brightens |

Feel free to adjust exact hexes for balance/accessibility, but keep this
relationship: **one warm terracotta anchor, one golden accent, one fresh
green counter-note, one playful pink pop, cream background, dark roast text.**

## Logo brief
- **Concept:** should visually nod to the "buddies" / matching idea, not just
  "coffee cup." Strong directions: two coffee cups overlapping or clinking
  like a toast; steam from a cup curling into a shape suggesting two people
  or a shuffle/pairing motif; a coffee bean split into two halves coming
  together.
- **Style:** flat, friendly vector illustration — rounded shapes, warm and
  approachable, not photorealistic, not a gradient-heavy tech logo. Should
  feel hand-friendly (a bit like a nice indie coffee shop's sign), not
  corporate-geometric.
- **Must work as:** a small favicon/app icon (readable at 32px), a standalone
  mark without the wordmark, and in a single colour (for stamps, embossing,
  etc.).
- **Avoid:** literal steaming-cup clip art, coffee-bean cliché with no twist,
  anything that reads as a big coffee chain, dark/moody colouring.
- **Wordmark:** pair with a warm, rounded serif or friendly sans for "Brew
  Buddies" — the site currently uses **Fraunces** (display serif) and **Work
  Sans** (body); keep that pairing in mind if the AI is generating type
  alongside the mark, since it should still feel like the same family on the
  website.

## Deliverable ask
1. A primary logo mark (icon + wordmark together).
2. A standalone icon/mark (for favicon/app icon use), single colour and full
   colour versions.
3. The finalised colour palette as hex values, in the roles above (primary,
   secondary, two accents, background, ink).
